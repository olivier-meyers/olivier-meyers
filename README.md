### Olivier Meyers

Geophysicist moving into natural catastrophe modelling. Built two open Swiss cat models. Research Assistant at the Swiss Seismological Service (ETH Zürich), preparing a journal paper from my MSc thesis on deep-learning rockfall detection related to the 2025 Blatten collapse. MSc Applied Geophysics (ETH Zürich, TU Delft, RWTH Aachen), 2026. Based in Zürich.

- [chstorm](https://github.com/olivier-meyers/chstorm): open windstorm catastrophe model for Swiss buildings, calibrated to Lothar (1999), with EP curves and a cat XL layer price
- [chquake](https://github.com/olivier-meyers/chquake): open earthquake catastrophe model for Swiss residential buildings (benchmark against ERM-CH23 in progress).
- [Blatten rockfall detection](https://github.com/olivier-meyers/blatten-rockfall-detection): finding rockfall in continuous seismic data
- [Bedretto seismic denoising](https://github.com/olivier-meyers/bedretto-seismic-denoising): deep-learning denoising of acoustic-emission recordings from the Bedretto Underground Laboratory
